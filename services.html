The process

Questions

Before you book

Do I have to give up carbs?

No. You will learn what carbohydrates do, how much you personally need, and how to fit them into your week. Cutting out whole food groups is exactly the kind of advice that has left so many women confused and stuck.

Will you put me on a meal plan?

Not in the way you are imagining. A printed plan works until the day you eat something that is not on it. I teach you the reasoning (portions, protein, labels) so you can make good decisions in a restaurant, at a family gathering, or on a bad week.

What if I am on a weight-loss injection already?

Bring it to the call. That is a conversation to have with me and with your doctor. My coaching is built to work without injections, but I am not going to make medical decisions for you, and I will always tell you when something belongs with your physician.

Do I need a gym?

No. The program is nutrition coaching. Movement helps, and we will talk about it, but you will not be asked to spend hours you do not have in a gym.

How much does it cost?

I share the investment on the consultation call, once I know which program actually fits you. The call itself is free and there is no obligation to continue.

What happens after sixteen weeks?

You keep going, on your own. That is the whole design. My goal is never to make you dependent on me; it is to give you the knowledge and confidence to manage your nutrition for life.
